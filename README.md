# Drop Chance Forever

Real drop chances for **WoW Forever**, shared by everyone.

- **The addon** (`DropChanceForever`): counts the mobs you kill and what they drop, and shows the chance on
  mob and item tooltips: everyone's numbers and yours. `/dcf` opens a small search window.
- **The Companion** (Windows, optional): sends your counts and keeps everyone's numbers fresh in the game.
  Each addon release also ships the numbers as they were at that time.
- **The cloud mailbox** (`worker/`): a Cloudflare Worker that keeps the uploads until the home server
  collects them, and hands out the combined numbers.

Made by Marijan. Not made by or connected to Blizzard.
